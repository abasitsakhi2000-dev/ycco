OUR STORY

From Kabul to the World

Founded on August 7, 2015, in Afghanistan, YCCO began as a response to decades of conflict that left young people, especially women, with little access to education, justice, or economic opportunity.

A small team of committed youth created YCCO to change this reality through capacity building, advocacy, and empowerment. What started as a grassroots initiative in Kabul soon expanded across provinces.

Over time, YCCO realized that these challenges were not unique to Afghanistan. Around the world, young people faced the same barriers to education, employment, and equality.

Today, YCCO has grown into a global platform, connecting youth, women, governments, and industries across borders. From a local niche to an international movement, YCCO continues its mission: ensuring that every young person has the skills, opportunities, and rights to lead change.

Vision

A world where every young person and woman has equal access to education, empowerment, and opportunities, enabling them to lead change and build inclusive, peaceful, and prosperous societies.

Mission

To empower youth and women globally through education, advocacy, and economic development. YCCO builds capacity, fosters leadership, and drives systemic change by connecting communities across borders.

WHAT WE DO

Programs & Services


Aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals — SDG 4, SDG 5, SDG 8, and SDG 16.

Education & Capacity Building

Certification courses, workshops, digital learning platforms, and Training of Trainers (ToT) for educators and community leaders.

Youth Employment & Entrepreneurship

Mentorship programs, internship placements, and access to global business networks for economic self-reliance.

Advocacy & Policy Development

Global advocacy campaigns on youth rights, gender equality, and policy dialogue with governments and international organizations.

Research & Knowledge Sharing

Cross-border studies on youth empowerment, peacebuilding, global best-practice mapping and data-driven advocacy.

Peacebuilding & Human Rights

Training on conflict resolution, democratic governance, women's rights awareness, and community mobilization for social cohesion.

Global Networking & Partnerships

Platforms connecting universities, NGOs, governments, and industries for cross-border collaboration and youth leadership exchanges.
