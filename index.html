OUR STORY

From Kabul to the World

Founded on August 7, 2015, in Afghanistan, YCCO began as a response to decades of conflict that left young people, especially women, with little access to education, justice, or economic opportunity.

A small team of committed youth created YCCO to change this reality through capacity building, advocacy, and empowerment. What started as a grassroots initiative in Kabul soon expanded across provinces.

Over time, YCCO realized that these challenges were not unique to Afghanistan. Around the world, young people faced the same barriers to education, employment, and equality.

Today, YCCO has grown into a global platform, connecting youth, women, governments, and industries across borders. From a local niche to an international movement, YCCO continues its mission: ensuring that every young person has the skills, opportunities, and rights to lead change.

Vision

A world where every young person and woman has equal access to education, empowerment, and opportunities, enabling them to lead change and build inclusive, peaceful, and prosperous societies.

Mission

To empower youth and women globally through education, advocacy, and economic development. YCCO builds capacity, fosters leadership, and drives systemic change by connecting communities across borders.

Articles of Association

The following is a summary of YCCO's founding statutes. YCCO was originally established in Afghanistan and has since grown into an international non-profit organisation. Its administrative office is registered in Kabul, Afghanistan.

§ 1 Name, Registered Office & Fiscal Year
The organisation bears the name "Youth Commitment for Change Organisation" (YCCO). It was founded on August 7, 2015, in Kabul, Afghanistan. Its current administrative registered office is in Kabul, Afghanistan. The fiscal year runs from January 1 to December 31.

§ 2 Purpose
YCCO pursues exclusively non-profit, charitable purposes. Its mission is to empower youth and women globally through education, advocacy, economic development, and peacebuilding — with a commitment to the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs 4, 5, 8, and 16). YCCO promotes international understanding, tolerance, and equal opportunities regardless of nationality, gender, or background.

§ 3 International Operations
YCCO operates across 25+ countries through partnerships with universities, NGOs, governments, and civil society. Its activities are carried out by staff, volunteers, and partners worldwide. All operations align with YCCO's non-profit mission.

§ 4 Membership
Any individual or organisation sharing YCCO's values and goals may apply for membership. Applications are submitted in writing to the board and are subject to approval.

§ 5 Governing Bodies
YCCO is governed by: (1) the General Assembly of members, and (2) the Board of Directors, consisting of the Chairperson, Deputy Chairperson, and Treasurer. The organisation is legally represented jointly by two board members.

§ 6 Dissolution
In the event of dissolution, all remaining assets shall be transferred to a recognised non-profit organisation pursuing comparable goals in education, youth empowerment, or international development.
